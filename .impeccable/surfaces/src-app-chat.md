# NEXA app surface

Scope: the /chat working shell, settings and provider flows (Operate). The landing page at / inherits this world (Persuade).
Audience and job: key holders chatting for hours, switching models, on desktop and on a phone, wanting to see where requests go.
Memorable moment: the wire under the composer that names the exact host a message will reach.
Unresolved: real domain, real social image.
History: the founder rejected the first world (Prussian blue cyanotype) and pinned "no blue", asking for UI UX Pro Max, 21st and phone compatibility. A user-pinned direction beats the roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm, quiet chat client with one precious colour, which shows the route of every message. It refuses blue, the grey vendor chat clone and the glowing AI gradient.

OWN-WORLD: Warm stone neutrals with a gold accent, from the UI UX Pro Max "premium dark plus gold accent" palette. Gold carries the primary action, focus and selection. Hairline seams instead of heavy shadow. Destructive actions stand apart and stay outline until focused. Tabular figures for numbers. Five user-selectable palettes (gold, emerald, violet, coral, mono) derive their neutrals from one hue and tint, so each reads as one material.

STORY: The visitor sees a calm, familiar chat, notices the wire names their own provider host, understands nothing sits between them, and keeps it open, on any device.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 280px sidebar, conversation column centred at about 760px, floating composer with attach, textarea, model selector and send, the wire under it. On a phone: drawer sidebar, bottom sheets for pickers and menus, composer above the keyboard. Landing: the tagline left, the real interface right, the wire across the page.

FORM: User-pinned, stone and gold. Started from candidate 3 of 7 and replaced at the founder's request. seed:949658580b8f4fbd

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
